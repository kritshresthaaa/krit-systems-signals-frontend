01 • STRATEGIC INSIGHT & POSITIONING

Flipping “Engagement Maximization” into Log-Off Advocacy

College students spend an average of 7+ hours daily on screens and have developed automatic banner blindness toward institutional flyers and preachy wellness apps. By adopting the visual language of Windows XP dialogs, SaaS pricing parodies, and live Screen Time alerts, TOUCH GRASS™ disarms skepticism with humor—earning organic shares because it respects student intelligence.

02 • HIGHER-ED IT & CAMPUS ADAPTATION

Applying This Framework to University IT Customer Relationships

University IT departments often struggle to get students to read cybersecurity alerts, software availability updates, or digital detox initiatives because emails feel overly bureaucratic. This campaign proves how relatable OS humor + crisp editorial design can turn routine campus tech messaging (e.g., “Digital Detox Week,” “2FA Checkups,” or “Free Campus Software vs. Paid Subscriptions”) into high-engagement student touchpoints.
